### Implemented

- `kernel-patches/0009`: `EXPORT_SYMBOL_GPL(aarch64_insn_copy)` and `EXPORT_SYMBOL_GPL(aarch64_insn_set)`. `make kernel-tree`:
  9 patches, 0001-0008 keep their commit ids. Both guest kernels rebuilt in place (no warning; the symbols are in `Module.symvers`).
- `kjit_glue.c`: the per-fragment execmem allocation, the A14 colour offset (`KJIT_IMAGE_COLOR_*`, `kjit_image_installs`), `f->alloc`,
  the per-fragment `set_memory_rox` and `execmem_free` are gone. New: 2 MiB chunks (`execmem_alloc(EXECMEM_BPF)`, `memset32` BRK,
  icache flush, `set_memory_rox` once), a 16-byte-granule bitmap per chunk (`bitmap_find_next_zero_area`, first fit, new chunks at the
  list tail), `kjit_arena_lock` (mutex), `KJIT_ARENA_MAX_CHUNKS` 64, the `kvmalloc` build buffer, `aarch64_insn_copy`, BRK fill
  (`aarch64_insn_set`) on free and on a failed install, empty-chunk `execmem_free`, gauges `arena_chunks` and `arena_bytes` in the
  stats file after the link gauges, and `WARN_ON`s for both gauges and the chunk list at module exit. Header comment and lifetimes
  section updated. `runtime/`: only a comment (`mod.rs`).
- `tests/guest/arena_churn.c`, registered in `run-k2.sh` (so it runs in K2 and, with the auto mode, in K3): 4 worker processes, each with
  4 hot loops and 6 callees in pages of its own, padded to fragments of 0.1 to 6 KB, `bl` links between them; a second thread per worker
  flips random pages exec-only <-> read-exec / `MADV_DONTNEED` and requests the new translation. A loop adds its own constant, the
  callees add 1..6, so a slot with another fragment's code or overlapped by another slot gives a wrong sum or a crash. Then the first callee is
  rewritten in place and the next batch of every loop must see it. With `KJIT_EXPECT=arena` the parent (which runs no translated code)
  samples the gauges, requires >= 100 installs and invalidations and the arena in use, and after the last worker exits polls (30 s)
  until `arena_chunks` and `arena_bytes` are 0.
- `run-k2.sh`: the `arena_churn` line; the placement guard comment now cites A17. `ub-standard.sh` prints `arena_chunks`,
  `arena_bytes` on every run line. `tests/guest/host/ub-arena.py`: the paired tables below.

Install context, free context (each must be sleepable, hold no spinlock, interrupts on): `kjit_install()` is reached only through
`kjit_rs_translate()`, from the debugfs `translate` write, the debugfs `translate_svc_sites` write, and the auto-mode `task_work`
(exit-to-user, inside the hook SRCU read section, preemptible). The release (BRK fill, bitmap) runs only in `kjit_frag_free_work()`
(workqueue `kjit` via `queue_rcu_work`, process context) and in the failed-install path of `kjit_install()`. The SRCU callback
(BH context) and `kjit_mm_flush_locked()` (spinlock) only queue the free work. `kjit-guest-debug` (`DEBUG_ATOMIC_SLEEP`, lockdep) showed no
splat in any gate.

### Decisions not in the contract

- The fragment is added to `kjit_all_frags` under `kmm->lock`, right before `hash_add_rcu`, no longer before the lock is taken. A failed
  install is therefore never on the extable list: its slot is filled and released at once, its `kjit_frag` freed directly (no RCU work).
- After the copy the slot is compared with the build buffer (`memcmp`, `-EIO` and the slot released on a difference): the patcher's
  `copy_to_kernel_nofault` reports nothing, and what the verifier accepted must be what is in the slot.
- A new chunk gets `flush_icache_range` after its BRK fill, so a branch into a never-written slot cannot run a stale icache line of the
  pages' previous use.
- Only `code + extable` bytes are written; the tail up to the 16-byte granule stays BRK. An image larger than a chunk is `-ENOSPC`.
- If `aarch64_insn_set` refuses a slot at free (misaligned, impossible for a slot from the arena), the slot is leaked (`WARN_ON_ONCE`,
  visible in `arena_bytes` and the exit `WARN_ON`), not reused.

### Conclusion

- A17 removes 0.56-0.98 us per request from KJIT on against main, in every cell, all 5 boots, both KPTI settings (04:15 predicted
  0.75-1.15 for the prototype). KJIT's cost over KJIT off drops from 0.90-1.78 to 0.31-0.77 us per request.
- **KJIT on still does not beat KJIT off in any cell**: A17 - off is positive in 60 of 60 boot-cells (5/5 boots). The nearest are
  KPTI forced on, GET, -d 1/64/1024 (+0.31, +0.33, +0.31 us). KPTI forced on lowers A17 - off by 0.0-0.3 us (off itself gets 0.1-0.2 us
  dearer by 0.09-0.22, A17 on moves by -0.07..+0.04). On this platform a null syscall costs about 130 ns (`svc_bench`, 04:15) and a
  request runs 2.0 syscalls in the kernel (`syscalls_in_kernel` per request, 3.05 fragment entries and 2.05 hook calls), so the syscall
  entry saving is bounded by about 0.26 us per request (more with KPTI) while KJIT's own remaining cost is 0.3 us or more.
- Leads, not implemented: (1) conditions with dearer syscall entry than M1/HVF with KPTI (mitigations that cost > 0.3 us per
  syscall: the same code would win there); (2) 3.05 fragment entries per request, so the remaining 0.3-0.8 us is 0.1-0.25 us per
  entry (a division, not a measurement of the entry): prologue/epilogue and hook-call cost per entry, or fewer entries per request; (3) the hot set (a subset of the ~300
  fragments, its size not measured) is interleaved with cold ones by install order; placing hot fragments contiguously (a separate hot chunk or relocation by
  hotness) is the next layout step if the dose-response of 04:15 continues.

### Not verified

- The mechanism (L1I conflict) is still inferred; no PMU in the guest. One core type (M1 under HVF), 4 vCPUs, redis SET/GET only.
- Chunk cap path (`-ENOSPC` at 64 chunks), the `set_memory_rox` failure path, `-EIO`/NULL paths of the copy, a 16 KiB-page kernel and
  `rodata=full` with KPTI (the gates ran with the default `rodata`; the measurement with `rodata=off`) were not exercised.
- Cost of the IPIs per install and per free (a process exit with ~300 fragments does ~300 icache-flush IPI rounds in the free work);
  chunk churn when the last fragment of an otherwise empty arena is retired and the next one is installed (2 MiB allocated, filled and
  made ROX again).
- Main's memory footprint was not measured in this run (04:15: about 160 pages for 296 fragments, same order).
- The unexplained `translate_ok` difference above; the 0.55-0.98 split between density and the IPI/copy path; module unload with a
  non-empty arena (the exit `WARN_ON` did not fire in any gate).
