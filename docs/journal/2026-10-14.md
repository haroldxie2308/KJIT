### Decision not in the contract: the alias is unmapped, not read-only

The contract says the helper makes the alias read-only. No in-tree entry point does that for this block: `change_memory_common()` is the
only route to a read-only linear alias (`set_memory_ro` -> `__change_memory_common` on `area->pages[idx]`) and it refuses
`VM_ALLOW_HUGE_VMAP` areas; `__change_memory_common()` is static in `pageattr.c`. Making it callable means a new export or a new function
in `arch/arm64/mm/` (a contract change in mm). `set_direct_map_valid_noflush(page, nr, false)` exists for exactly this, one call for the
512 pages (it flushes the TLB range itself on arm64), and `vm_reset_perms()` already passes through that state on every
VM_FLUSH_RESET_PERMS free (`set_direct_map_invalid_noflush`, flush, `set_direct_map_default_noflush`), so the free path restores it with no
extra code. A write through the alias faults either way (W^X holds); a read faults too, where A17's chunk alias stays readable. Nothing
legitimate reads the alias of vmalloc memory. Proposed contract edit: "Linear alias: ... the helper unmaps the linear alias of every page
(read and write fault; `set_direct_map_valid_noflush`) before it returns the block"; same wording in patch list item 10 ("an unmapped
linear alias under `rodata=full`"). If read-only is wanted, it is an extra mm patch.

### Conclusion

- The implementation works as the contract says, with one deviation: the alias is unmapped, not read-only (above). Page-table evidence:
  PMD leaf, alias invalid, 12 + 12 failure/success cycles and a memory fill without a fault, the fallback chunk with a read-only alias.
- On the UB workload (server never idle, `--threads 2`, 500000 requests per run) the PMD chunk saves **0.01-0.22 us per request (cell
  medians; KPTI off -0.12..-0.22, KPTI on -0.01..-0.17); 5 of 32 boot-cells have a positive median**. The 22:54 experiment measured -0.05..-0.18
  per cell (median -0.12), 0 of 16 boots positive; the KPTI-off cells agree, the KPTI-on SET cells are smaller here (-0.01, -0.05, intervals
  contain 0). A19 still costs +0.27..+0.63 us per request over KJIT off.
- **At low client counts the saving is large**: -2.1 us per request at `-c 1` (GET and SET, 0 of 4 boots positive), i.e. 46% / 40% of A17's +4.7 /
  +5.4 us gap to KJIT off (A19: +2.9 / +3.3), and -0.27..-0.29 at `-c 10` (29% / 28% of the +0.9 / +1.0 gap). That is consistent with the
  exp/c1 finding that the gap is a per-wake-up cold start of the translated code, and it makes the iTLB/walk footprint of the chunk a large
  part of that cold start (about 2 of the 4.8 us per wake-up of the exp/c1 fit); no PMU confirms it. The rest (+2.9 us at `-c 1`) remains.

### Not verified

- The timing runs use `rodata=off` (the `kpti=1` boot hang of `ub-run.sh`), so the alias unmapping and the 512 `set_direct_map` PTE updates
  are not in the timed path (they are in chunk creation, once per 2 MiB, and in the gates under the default `rodata`). Chunk creation time
  was not measured.
- The order-9 failure of the helper was forced by a knob after the allocation, not provoked by a fragmented memory; the `page_order != 9`
  branch (vmalloc's own 4 KiB fallback) never ran.
- Multi-chunk arenas: every run had one chunk. A16 links across chunks up to 128 MiB apart: `link_out_of_range` was 0 in the K2 stats and the
  measurement runs, no cross-chunk case was constructed.
- The experiment ran on kjit-guest only; kjit-guest-debug (KASAN generic, lockdep, DEBUG_ATOMIC_SLEEP) ran the product gates with the
  helper and `arena_chunks_pmd` 1, but its alias was not probed.
- One CPU (M1 under HVF), 4 vCPUs, redis 7.0.15; `-c 1`/`-c 10` KPTI off only, `-d 1` only, CPU from `sum_exec_runtime` in the guest, not
  a PMU. `serial.log`s of the runs contain the other agents' load only through `host.txt`.
